Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 27, 2026  ·  Effective: March 27, 2026

Tap Cricket

Placeholder Pvt Ltd ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Tap Cricket mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have over your data.

By downloading or using the App you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Display name and avatar you choose during onboarding
  • Organisation name (optional)
  • Email address and profile photo — only when you sign in with Google or Apple

Information generated by your activity:

  • Match results, scores, wickets, and difficulty levels
  • City conquest records and throne holder status
  • Tournament history and league standings
  • Daily play streaks and achievement unlocks
  • Season points and leaderboard ranking

Technical information collected automatically:

  • Device push notification token (for sending notifications you have opted into)
  • Crash reports and error logs (via Sentry)
  • Anonymised usage events such as screen views and feature interactions (via PostHog)
  • Device type, operating system version, and app version

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, microphone, or camera data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the App and provide its core features (matches, leaderboards, city modes, tournaments)
  • To display your name, avatar, and scores on global leaderboards and city throne pages
  • To send you push notifications about streaks, daily challenges, and city events — only if you grant permission
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs
  • To understand which features are used so we can improve the App
  • To maintain your account and sync data across devices

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — database and authentication infrastructure. Data is stored on servers hosted by Supabase Inc. supabase.com/privacy
  • Google Sign-In — optional authentication. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple Sign-In — optional authentication. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) — delivery of push notifications on Android. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) — delivery of push notifications on iOS. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Sentry — crash reporting and error monitoring. sentry.io/privacy
  • PostHog — anonymised product analytics. posthog.com/privacy
  • Expo (by Expo Inc.) — app development platform. expo.dev/privacy

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not share your personal information with advertisers. Your display name and scores are visible to other users on leaderboards and city throne pages — this is a core feature of the App. You can change your display name at any time in the App settings.

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Match history is retained for up to 12 months on our servers. Anonymised analytics data may be retained for up to 24 months. If you delete your account (via Settings → Delete Account in the App), your personal data is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Tap Cricket is rated for users aged 4 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email below and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — update your display name or avatar within the App
  • Deletion — delete your account via Settings → Delete Account, or contact us
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format
  • Opt-out of notifications — disable notifications via your device settings at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at placeholder@gmail.com.

8. Security

We implement commercially reasonable security measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit and row-level security policies on our database. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, through an in-app notice. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
