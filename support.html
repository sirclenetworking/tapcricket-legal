What you can contact us about

Reach out to support@cocial.in for any of the following, or anything else related to the app:

  • Bugs, crashes, or gameplay issues
  • Problems with sign in, syncing, or your account
  • Questions about matchmaking, private rooms, or ranked matches
  • Reporting inappropriate behaviour or content from another player
  • Account deletion requests
  • Requests to access, correct, or export your data
  • Billing or purchase questions
  • General feedback and feature suggestions
2

Requesting account deletion by email

You can delete your account directly in the app at any time, or you can email us and we will take care of it for you. If you email us, please include the following so we can find and remove your account quickly:

  1. The display name shown on your profile in the app
  2. The email address used to sign in, if you signed in with Google or Apple
  3. A short note asking for your account and data to be deleted
Once we confirm the request, your personal data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days, in line with our Privacy Policy.
3

Before you email us

A few quick checks that solve most issues instantly:

  • Make sure you are on the latest version of the app from the App Store or Play Store
  • Try closing and reopening the app if a match or screen feels stuck
  • Check your internet connection for sync or multiplayer issues
  • Restart your device if notifications are not arriving as expected

Still stuck? Email us and we will help you sort it out.
